# Scientific and implementation limits

- Preprocessing consumes supplied crops. No recovered executable16uniform-frame,
  MTCNN/largest-face video extraction/selection history is authenticated or
  provided. RGB conversion, resizing and normalization of existing crops are
  implemented; this is not the complete Section3.1 pipeline.
- Symmetric Db2 extension, bilinear coefficient resize, SGFE stabilization/max
  normalization, SE hidden size and sigmoid projection are disclosed correction
  choices where historical details are not established. No pooling is presented
  as genuine Db2. Live PyWavelets parity remains dependency-blocked.
- Corrected backbones support Xception, InceptionV3 and MobileNetV1 only.
  Table2's four additional benchmark architectures and a complete Table7
  validation-grid experiment runner are absent. Configurable feature-layer
  parameters alone are not a completed sensitivity experiment.
- The manuscript indexes MobileNetV1/InceptionV3/Xception as1/2/3; the code fixes
  Xception/InceptionV3/MobileNetV1 order. Model-name binding is mandatory; numeric
  weight vectors must not be transferred positionally without the permutation.
- The manuscript states source-validation ACC/Fake F1/Fake AUC and fixed0.50;
  corrected metrics/fusion implement these definitions. Historical scripts used
  different selection/weighting and sometimes artificial metric adjustments.
  Existing summary CSVs are not predictions and cannot authenticate Tables5–6.
- Celeb has a confirmed current content-overlap pair, unresolved similar pairs
  and missing original video/person/frame joins. FF++official sequence allocation
  and upstream-video independence are different; source grouping choice remains
  unresolved. No new split or implicit exclusion is activated here.
- Manuscript Adam/LR/batch/regularization values can be explicitly configured but
  are not authenticated historical run settings. Default-free config validation
  does not establish that the paper's training occurred. Full training, optimizer
  resumption, clean installs, native-Windows/GPU runs and full real-checkpoint
  evaluation are unvalidated.
- Frame metrics/video means are implemented. Cross-dataset manuscript results
  are frame-only; optional generic video summaries are extra functionality.
  JPEG codec/resize and Gaussian RNG/clipping settings are new documented
  choices, not recovered original degradation histories.
- Grad-CAM and LIME require reviewed model/target/input range and, for LIME,
  superpixel segments. Pre/post activation are distinct settings; no TCRF
  classifier is used. An exact source/cohort for manuscript Figures8–9 remains
  unverified; full photographic panel reproduction is not supplied.
- The public package excludes the six-region semantic attribution implementation,
  mask utilities, regional calculations and regional plotting. It cannot
  independently reproduce that analysis. Obtain the original implementation
  from its author subject to permission and applicable licensing conditions.
- Tables1–8 lack complete authenticated per-experiment inputs/checkpoints/configs
  and predictions. Table8's external comparison values require primary-source
  checking; no third-party detector reimplementation/result is supplied.

Do not change manuscript values or fabricate predictions to close these gaps.
Do not automatically tighten splits or alter architecture/threshold/weights.
Corrected scientific training/evaluation and claims of manuscript reproduction
remain NO-GO. The clean data-free software can be reviewed independently, but
actual publication requires owner rights/license and final approval.
